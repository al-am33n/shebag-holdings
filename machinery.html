Heavy Machinery & Equipment

Supply, sales, and leasing of heavy-duty machinery for Nigeria's construction, mining, and agricultural sectors — backed by maintenance and operator support.

Equipment We Supply

Machinery & Equipment

From earthmoving to harvesting — a fleet of heavy equipment to power your operations. Select an item to learn more.

🚜

Tractors — Agricultural & Utility

Agricultural and utility tractors across a range of horsepower classes — for farming, haulage, loading, and general site work. Supplied with parts support and servicing suited to Nigerian operating conditions.

🏗️

Excavators — Earthmoving & Construction

Crawler and wheeled excavators for construction, mining, and earthmoving projects. Available for sale or lease, with operator training and ongoing maintenance support.

🚛

Dump Trucks — Haulage & Tipping

Heavy-duty tipper and articulated dump trucks for hauling aggregates, ore, overburden, and construction materials across demanding sites and long distances.

⛏️

Wagon Drill — Drilling & Blasting

Pneumatic wagon and track drills for quarrying, mining, and blast-hole drilling — rugged rigs built for continuous, high-output operation in tough terrain.

🌾

Harvesters — Mechanised Agriculture

Combine and crop harvesters that mechanise large-scale agricultural production — cutting labour costs while improving yields and turnaround during harvest.
