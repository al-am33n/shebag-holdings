Oil & Gas Division

Shebag's founding sector — providing oil field services, supplies, and technical support across Nigeria's energy industry since 1988.

Founding Sector

Where the Shebag story began.

When Shebag Holdings was incorporated in 1988, the company was founded with a singular purpose: to indigenise oil field services and engineering supplies — sectors then dominated by foreign multinationals. For more than three decades, oil & gas has remained at the core of our business.

Today, Shebag is a NIPEX-registered vendor serving operators, EPC contractors, and regulators across Nigeria's upstream, midstream, and downstream value chains.

Products Marketed

Petroleum & Petrochemical Products

Shebag markets a diversified portfolio of refined petroleum products and petrochemicals across Nigeria's downstream sector. Select a product to learn more.

PMS — Premium Motor Spirit (Petrol)

Premium Motor Spirit, commonly known as petrol, is the primary fuel for spark-ignition engines across Nigeria's transport and commercial sectors. Shebag supplies PMS to the standards and specifications set by the relevant downstream regulators, supporting distribution to marketers, filling stations, and bulk end-users.

AGO — Automotive Gas Oil (Diesel)

Automotive Gas Oil, or diesel, powers heavy-duty engines, industrial machinery, generators, and haulage fleets. Shebag markets AGO to industrial consumers, power generation operators, construction firms, and the logistics sector — with a focus on consistent quality and reliable supply for operations that cannot afford downtime.

DPK — Dual Purpose Kerosene

Dual Purpose Kerosene serves both domestic and aviation-adjacent applications, from household heating and cooking to industrial use. Shebag supplies DPK to distributors and commercial consumers, maintaining the handling and storage standards this refined product requires.

LPG — Liquefied Petroleum Gas (Cooking Gas)

Liquefied Petroleum Gas is central to Nigeria's clean-cooking transition, replacing traditional biomass fuels in homes and businesses. Shebag participates in the LPG value chain — supplying to distributors, plant operators, and bulk off-takers — supporting wider access to cleaner household and commercial energy.

Fuel Oil — Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO / LPFO)

Fuel Oil is a heavy residual product used for industrial heat and power generation, marine bunkering, and large-scale boiler and furnace operations. Shebag markets fuel oil to manufacturers, industrial plants, and power producers requiring a dependable energy source for high-demand thermal applications.

Polypropylene — Petrochemical Polymer

Polypropylene is a versatile thermoplastic polymer and a key petrochemical feedstock for manufacturing — used in packaging, textiles, automotive components, containers, and consumer goods. Shebag supplies polypropylene resin to Nigeria's manufacturing and plastics-processing industries, connecting local producers to reliable raw-material sources.

Sulphur — Industrial & Agricultural Grade

Sulphur is a fundamental industrial raw material, essential to fertilizer production, chemical manufacturing, and a range of processing industries. Shebag markets sulphur to agricultural input producers and industrial consumers, supporting both the fertilizer value chain and downstream chemical applications.

Urea — Nitrogen Fertilizer

Urea is the world's most widely used nitrogen fertilizer and a cornerstone of agricultural productivity. Bridging Shebag's oil & gas and agriculture interests, we market urea to distributors, agro-dealers, and large-scale farming operations — supporting food security through improved crop yields across Nigeria.
